About

I am currently pursuing a master's degree in Control Science and Engineering in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University. Before joining Tsinghua, I received my B.Eng. in Aircraft Control and Information Engineering from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, where I ranked first in my major.

My work lies at the intersection of perception, representation learning, predictive world models, and reinforcement learning. I am particularly interested in incorporating high-level semantic knowledge into models of physical dynamics, and in using these representations to improve planning and control in embodied systems.

02

Research Interests

Semantic 3D Representation Learning

Learning structured and generalizable representations of physical environments by combining 3D perception, neural representations, vision-language models, and semantic scene understanding.

World Models for Embodied Agents

Developing predictive latent models that capture physical dynamics together with high-level semantic information for planning and decision-making.

Learning-Based Autonomous Systems

Integrating perception, representation learning, and reinforcement learning to create agents that adapt to complex environments, with longer-term interests in robot learning and simulation-to-real transfer.

Selected Research Projects

February 2026 – presentProject Lead

AutoPlanax: Agentic Framework for Automated RL Research

Designed an LLM-agent workflow for hypothesis generation, reward reflection, reinforcement-learning experiments, automated evaluation, and history-aware feedback. The framework includes staged permission controls, early exit, and automatic Git rollback.

  • Ran 68 automated training-and-evaluation trials in the reported simulation study.
  • The best-performing policy achieved a quaternion geodesic tracking error of 20°, compared with more than 90° at baseline.
