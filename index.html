About

I am pursuing a master's degree in Control Science and Engineering in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, with graduation expected in July 2027. I received my B.Eng. in Aircraft Control and Information Engineering from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, where I ranked first in my major.

My recent work spans multimodal semantic distillation for latent world models, large-scale parallel simulation, goal-conditioned flight control, and LLM-driven reinforcement-learning experiments. I also worked on Agent Skill mining and evaluation at ByteDance. I am seeking PhD opportunities in agentic RL, world models, and recursive self-improvement.

02

Research Interests

Agentic RL & Automated Research

Building LLM agents that generate hypotheses, revise rewards and code, schedule reinforcement-learning experiments, and use evaluation feedback to guide the next iteration.

World Models & Physical Intelligence

Internalizing multimodal semantic priors into latent predictive models while preserving dynamics prediction, to support planning and closed-loop control in embodied systems.

Recursive Self-Improvement

Exploring agents that iteratively improve their research workflows through reward reflection, in-context evolutionary search, and feedback from training and evaluation.

Selected Research Projects

May – September 2026Core Team Member

WorldPrior: Multimodal Semantic Distillation for Latent World Models

Designed a semantic distillation framework that internalizes priors from a frozen vision-language model. Cosine alignment loss and a reference predictor retention loss enhance semantic understanding while preserving predictive capability.

  • Trained and evaluated Stable World Model on PushT, TwoRoom, Reacher, and Cube robotic-control tasks.
  • Achieved a 90% closed-loop control success rate; improved TwoRoom, Reacher, and Cube by 8, 4, and 6 percentage points, respectively.
February – September 2026Project Lead

AutoPlanax: Agentic Framework for Automated RL Research

Built a Python and LLM-API framework on Planax to connect hypothesis generation, code revision, training, evaluation, and historical feedback. The workflow combines reward reflection, in-context evolutionary search, staged permissions, early stopping, and automatic Git rollback.

  • Completed 100 automated experiments.
  • Reduced the best-performing policy's tracking error from more than 90° to 20°, measured as a geodesic angle in quaternion space.
March 2025 – March 2026Project Lead

Planax: Parallel Simulation & Reinforcement Learning for UAV Swarms

Led development of a high-fidelity JAX/XLA simulation platform that balances physical realism with large-scale reinforcement-learning throughput.

  • Implemented PPO, MAPPO, and hierarchical RL; completed 50-vs-50 UAV swarm self-play training.
  • Achieved more than 10× the simulation speed of NeuralPlane in the reported benchmark; one invention patent application was accepted for processing.

Code

March – May 2026Project Lead

G-EATS: Goal-Conditioned RL for Agile Fixed-Wing Control

Designed a goal-conditioned flight policy for direct actuator control in quaternion state space, with energy-aware PPO and closed-loop target-stream optimization.

  • Built a high-fidelity fixed-wing simulation environment for training and evaluation across multiple tasks and scenarios.
  • Demonstrated stable closed-loop control across agile maneuvering tasks and released the code.

Code

May 2025 – September 2026Project Lead

RL Sequential Data Collection & Simulation Replay Platform

Developed a data toolchain for a subproject of the national Science and Technology Innovation 2030 “Brain Science and Brain-Like Research” program.

  • Integrated Planax trajectories and SMAC training data with import validation, detailed queries, checkpoint archiving, and annotation management.
  • Recovered physical quantities from normalized six-degree-of-freedom observations and linked Tacview and StarCraft II for ACMI/SC2Replay management and visual replay.
September 2024 – March 2025Project Lead

Four-Reaction-Wheel Attitude Control for Micro- and Nanosatellites

Modeled a redundant pyramid-configured reaction-wheel system, compared PID/LQR/sliding-mode controllers, and built a closed-loop prototype using SolidWorks, Simscape, STM32G431, and 3D printing.

  • Demonstrated attitude maneuvers of up to 90° and robustness under a 150 mN·m disturbance.
  • Published a first-authored CCC paper and obtained one granted invention patent.
