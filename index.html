01 Manuscript in preparation

PrivilegedJEPA · Second author · 2026

Teacher-free semantic distillation for latent world models

PrivilegedJEPA injects multimodal priors from a frozen vision-language model into a V-JEPA 2 latent world model—strengthening high-level semantics while preserving predictive capability.

90% closed-loop success in the reported evaluation
